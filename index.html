ABOUT / 概要

Dragon Farm Sounds

Dragon Farm Sounds is an independent music and creative project led by RYU:GiN.

RYU:GiN works as a producer and AI music creator, focusing on songwriting, music production and creative projects.

Dragon Farm SoundsはRYU:GiNが主宰するインディペンデントな音楽・クリエイティブプロジェクトです。

RYU:GiNはプロデューサー／AI MUSIC CREATORとして、 作詞・作曲・音楽制作を中心にさまざまなクリエイティブ活動を行っています。

By combining different elements such as Japanese sounds, EDM beats, and AI technology, RYU:GiN creates a sound of his own.

From Trance, Reggae, and Hip-Hop to other genres, RYU:GiN is not bound by genre, freely turning each idea into music.

RYU:GiN / ARTIST STATEMENT

What RYU:GiN creates is, in essence, a kind of craft gin of sound. Just as gin is crafted by carefully blending diverse botanicals to create its own aroma and character, RYU:GiN precisely blends Japanese sounds, EDM beats and near-future AI technology to distill new sounds.

RYU:GiNが創り出すのは、まさに「音のクラフトジン」。 多彩なボタニカルを調合して香りを生み出すジンのように、 和の音色、EDMのビート、近未来AIテクノロジーを緻密にブレンドし、 新しいサウンドを蒸留しています。
